Common questions

What homeowners usually ask

How long will my kitchen be out of action?

For a typical kitchen, two to three days. Doors and drawer fronts come off on day one, the frames are sprayed in place, and everything is refitted on the final day. You keep the use of your sink and appliances throughout.

Will it chip?

Not in normal use. We finish in a two-pack lacquer, the same class of coating used on car bodywork and shopfitting, which is far harder wearing than the water-based paints usually sold for this job. It cleans with a damp cloth.

Can you match a colour I've already chosen?

Yes. Any RAL colour, and anything from Farrow & Ball, Little Greene or Dulux Heritage can be matched into the lacquer. Bring us a tin, a card or a code.

What about the mess?

Everything is masked and sheeted before a drop of paint is sprayed, and we vacuum the room before we leave. Where practical the doors go back to our unit and are sprayed in a dust-free environment, which keeps the disruption at your end to the frames only.

Do you spray worktops too?

We don't. Worktops take a level of abrasion that no sprayed coating survives long term, and we would rather tell you that than take the money and have you disappointed in a year.
